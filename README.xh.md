# BRASA Business

**Isoftware yeshishini yasimahla kunye nendawo yentengiso yabemi-elungiselelwe urhwebo ngalunye.**

[Vula i-BRASA Business](https://brasa.business/) · [BRASA World](https://brasa.world/) · [Imfundo](https://brasa.education/) · [Urhulumente](https://brasagovernment.net/)

I-BRASA Business inika abemi izixhobo ezisebenzayo zokuqalisa, ukusebenza, kunye nokukhulisa ishishini. Iqonga libandakanya imisebenzi emininzi kunye namacandelo ngelixa ugcina amava enxibelelene nemfundo, iinkonzo zikarhulumente, kunye nephepha lelizwe lommi ngamnye.

## Yintoni efunyanwa ngabemi

- Isoftware yeshishini yasimahla ngaphandle kovavanyo okanye umrhumo
- Izixhobo ezilungelelaniswe kubuchwephesha kunye namacandelo ahlukeneyo
- Isazisi se-BRASA ngenkqubo yesazisi ekwabelwana ngayo
- Intlawulo yomthengi ngokuthe ngqo kunokuba i-BRASA ibambe iimali zabarhwebi
- Uqhagamshelo lokufunda, iinkonzo zoluntu, kunye nendawo yentengiso ye-BRASA ebanzi

## Imigaqo yokusebenza

- I-BRASA ayihlawulisi abemi ukuba baqalise ukusebenzisa isoftware yeshishini.
- Isazisi senziwa ngenkonzo yokubhalisa ekwabelwana ngayo.
- I-BRASA ayihlali kwindlela yokuhlawula phakathi kweshishini kunye nomthengi walo.
- Intlawulo yelizwe ngalinye, irhafu, kunye namabango e-invoyisi kufuneka aqinisekiswe ngaphambi kokuba apapashwe.

## Ukufikelela kulwimi

IsiNgesi sihlala singumthombo ogciniweyo. Inguqulelo nganye epapashiweyo ifumana ifayile yayo kunye ne-URL yoluntu ukuze abemi bakwazi ukusebenzisa i-BRASA ngolwimi lwabo ngaphandle kokutshintsha umgangatho wokuhlela wesiNgesi.

## Qala ishishini

Ndwendwela [brasa.business](https://brasa.business/) ukuze uphonononge ubuchwephesha kwaye wenze iakhawunti yakho.

## Amanqaku omgcini

Indawo ibekwe njenge-HTML engatshintshiyo kunye neJavaScript. Utshintsho oluchaphazela isazisi, iintlawulo, i-invoyisi, okanye ukuthotyelwa kwelizwe elithile kufuna ukuqinisekiswa ngaphambi kokukhululwa.
