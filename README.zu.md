# BRASA Business

**Isofthiwe yebhizinisi yamahhala nendawo yemakethe yezakhamuzi—ehlelelwe uhwebo ngalunye.**

[Vula Ibhizinisi le-BRASA](https://brasa.business/) · [Umhlaba We-BRASA](https://brasa.world/) · [Imfundo](https://brasa.education/) · [Uhulumeni](https://brasagovernment.net/)

I-BRASA Business inikeza izakhamuzi amathuluzi angokoqobo okuqala, ukusebenza, nokukhulisa ibhizinisi. Inkundla ihlanganisa ubungcweti nemikhakha eminingi ngenkathi igcina ulwazi luxhumene nezemfundo, izinsiza zikahulumeni, kanye nekhasi lezwe lesakhamuzi ngasinye.

## Okuthola izakhamuzi

- Isofthiwe yebhizinisi yamahhala ngaphandle kwesilingo noma ukubhalisa
- Amathuluzi ajwayelane nobungcweti nemikhakha eyahlukene
- Umazisi we-BRASA ngohlelo lobunikazi obuhlanganyelwe besakhamuzi
- Inkokhelo yekhasimende eqondile kunokuba i-BRASA ibambe izimali zomthengisi
- Ukuxhumana nokufunda, izinsiza zomphakathi, kanye nemakethe ye-BRASA ebanzi

## Izimiso zokusebenza

- I-BRASA ayikhokhisi izakhamuzi ukuthi ziqale ukusebenzisa isofthiwe yebhizinisi.
- Ubunikazi budalwa ngesevisi yokubhalisa okwabelwana ngayo.
- I-BRASA ayihlali endleleni yokukhokha phakathi kwebhizinisi nekhasimende lalo.
- Izimangalo zenkokhelo eqondene nezwe elithile, intela, nama-invoyisi kufanele ziqinisekiswe ngaphambi kokushicilelwa.

## Ukufinyelela ulimi

IsiNgisi sihlala siwumthombo ogciniwe. Ukuhumusha ngakunye okushicilelwe kuthola ifayela lakho siqu kanye ne-URL yomphakathi ukuze izakhamuzi zikwazi ukusebenzisa i-BRASA ngolimi lwazo ngaphandle kokushintsha izinga lokuhlela lesiNgisi.

## Qala ibhizinisi

Vakashela i-[brasa.business](https://brasa.business/) ukuze uhlole ubungcweti futhi udale i-akhawunti yakho.

## Amanothi omgcini

Isayithi lisatshalaliswe njenge-HTML emile ne-JavaScript. Izinguquko ezithinta ubuwena, izinkokhelo, ama-invoyisi, noma ukuthobelana nezwe elithile zidinga ukuqinisekiswa ngaphambi kokukhishwa.

## BRASA namuhla

BRASA manje ixhumanisa izindawo zamazwe angu-198 neMfundo, uHulumeni kanye neBhizinisi. BRASA Business inezindawo eziyi-12 ezixhunyiwe: ukuthengisa namarisidi, CRM, impahla, abantu nokuqeqeshwa, amaholo namabhange, isikhathi, ukwesekwa komndeni, umsebenzi wezomthetho, amafomu kahulumeni, imibiko nendawo yokusebenza ehlanganyelwe. Ihlanganisa nebhuku lezimali elivulekile, izindlela zokusebenza eziqondisiwe nokuqhubeka ngaphandle kwe-inthanethi.

BRASA Financials ibala izinhlelo zokukhokha ezingama-385 ezivela kumarekhodi angama-395, ngezincazelo zemakethe eziyi-161 nezinhlobo zokukhokha ezingama-216: imali yeselula, ukudlulisa ngokushesha, QR, izikhwama zedijithali, amanethiwekhi emali, ukuthumela imali, open banking, amakhadi asekhaya kanye ne-USSD.

Ukufakwa ohlwini akusho ukuthi sekuvuliwe. Abahlinzeki abanelayisensi bahambisa imali; indlela ngayinye idinga ukubuyekezwa kwezwe nomthombo, ilayisensi, ukuhlanganiswa, ukuhlolwa kokukhokhelana kanye nemvume yokusebenza.
