# BRASA Business

**Software ya mahala ya kgwebo le mmaraka wa baahi—e hlophisitsweng bakeng sa kgwebo e nngwe le e nngwe.**

[Bula BRASA Business](https://brasa.business/) · [BRASA World](https://brasa.world/) · [Thuto](https://brasa.education/) · [Mmuso](https://brasagovernment.net/)

BRASA Business e fa baahi lisebelisoa tse sebetsang tsa ho qala, ho sebetsa le ho holisa khoebo. Sethala se akaretsa liporofeshene le makala a mangata ha se ntse se boloka boiphihlelo bo hokahane le thuto, lits'ebeletso tsa mmuso, le leqephe la naha ea moahi e mong le e mong.

## Seo baahi ba se amohelang

- Software ea mahala ea khoebo e se nang teko kapa ngoliso
- Lisebelisoa tse ikamahanyang le liprofeshene le mafapha a fapaneng
- ID ea BRASA ka mokhoa o arolelanoang oa boitsebiso ba baahi
- Tefo e tobileng ea bareki ho fapana le hore BRASA e ts'oare lichelete tsa barekisi
- Khokahano thutong, lits'ebeletso tsa sechaba, le 'maraka o pharalletseng oa BRASA

## Melao-motheo ea ts'ebetso

- BRASA ha e lefise baahi ho qala ho sebelisa software ea khoebo.
- Boitsebiso bo etsoa ka tšebeletso e arolelanoang ea ho ngolisa.
- BRASA ha e lule tseleng ea ho lefa lipakeng tsa khoebo le moreki oa eona.
- Litefiso tse ikhethileng tsa naha, lekhetho le li-invoice li tlameha ho netefatsoa pele ho phatlalatsoa.

## Ho fihlella puo

Senyesemane e ntse e le mohloli o tsitsitseng. Phetolelo e 'ngoe le e 'ngoe e hatisitsoeng e fumana faele ea eona le URL ea sechaba e le hore baahi ba sebelise BRASA ka puo ea bona ntle le ho fetola maemo a ho hlophisa Senyesemane.

## Qala khoebo

Etela [brasa.business](https://brasa.business/) ho hlahloba litsebi le ho theha akhaonto ea hau.

## Lintlha tsa mohlokomeli

Sebaka sa marang-rang se sebelisoa joalo ka HTML le JavaScript. Liphetoho tse amang boitsebahatso, litefo, li-invoice kapa melao e itseng ea naha li hloka netefatso pele e lokolloa.

## BRASA kajeno

BRASA jwale e hokahanya dibaka tsa dinaha tse 198 le Thuto, Mmuso le Kgwebo. BRASA Business e na le dikarolo tse 12 tse hokahaneng: thekiso le dirasiti, CRM, setoko, batho le thupelo, meputso le dibanka, nako, tshehetso ya lelapa, molao, diforomo tsa mmuso, ditlaleho le sebaka se kopanetsweng sa mosebetsi. E boetse e kenyelletsa lejara e bulehileng, ditsamaiso tse tataiswang le tshebetso ntle le inthanete.

BRASA Financials e thathamisa ditsamaiso tsa tefo tse 385 ho tswa direkotong tse 395, ka ditlhaloso tsa mebaraka tse 161 le mefuta ya tefo e 216: tjhelete ya mohala, phetisetso ya hanghang, QR, dipotlana tsa dijithale, marangrang a tjhelete, remitense, open banking, dikarete tsa lehae le USSD.

Ho ba lethathamong ha ho bolele hore ho se ho butswe. Bafani ba nang le laesense ke bona ba tsamaisang tjhelete; mokgwa o mong le o mong o hloka tlhahlobo ya naha le mohlodi, laesense, kopanyo, teko ya tefo le tumello ya tlhahiso.
