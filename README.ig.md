# BRASA Business

** Akụrụngwa azụmaahịa efu na ebe ahịa nwa amaala-ahaziri maka azụmaahịa ọ bụla.**

[Mepee BRASA Business](https://brasa.business/) · [BRASA World](https://brasa.world/) · [Agụmakwụkwọ](https://brasa.education/) · [Ọchịchị](https://brasagovernment.net/)

BRASA Business na-enye ụmụ amaala ngwá ọrụ bara uru iji malite, rụọ ọrụ na ịzụlite azụmahịa. Ikpo okwu na-ekpuchi ọtụtụ ọrụ na ngalaba ka ị na-echekwa ahụmịhe jikọtara na agụmakwụkwọ, ọrụ gọọmentị na ibe obodo onye ọ bụla.

## Ihe ụmụ amaala na-enweta

- Akụrụngwa azụmaahịa efu na-enweghị nnwale ma ọ bụ ndenye aha
- Ngwa ndị emegharịrị na ọrụ dị iche iche na ngalaba
- NJ BRASA site na sistemu njirimara nwa amaala ekekọrịtara
- Ụgwọ ndị ahịa ozugbo kama ijide ego ndị ahịa BRASA
- Njikọ na mmụta, ọrụ ọha na eze na ebe ahịa BRASA sara mbara

## Ụkpụrụ ọrụ

- BRASA anaghị akwụ ụmụ amaala ụgwọ ka ha malite iji ngwa ahịa azụmaahịa.
- A na-emepụta njirimara site na ọrụ ntinye aka nkekọrịta.
- BRASA anaghị anọdụ ala n'ụzọ ịkwụ ụgwọ n'etiti azụmahịa na onye ahịa ya.
- A ga-enyocharịrị ugwo kpọmkwem nke obodo, ụtụ isi, na akwụkwọ ọnụahịa tupu ebipụta ya.

## Nweta asụsụ

Bekee ka bụ isi mmalite echekwara. Ntụgharị asụsụ ọ bụla ebipụtara na-enweta faịlụ nke ya yana URL ọha ka ụmụ amaala nwee ike iji BRASA n'asụsụ ha na-agbanweghị ọkọlọtọ ndezi Bekee.

## Bido azụmahịa

Gaa na [brasa.business](https://brasa.business/) iji nyochaa ọrụ ma mepụta akaụntụ gị.

## ndetu ndenye

A na-ekesa saịtị ahụ dị ka HTML na Javascript kwụ ọtọ. Mgbanwe na-emetụta njirimara, ịkwụ ụgwọ, akwụkwọ ọnụahịa, ma ọ bụ nnabata mba kpọmkwem chọrọ nkwenye tupu ahapụ ya.

## BRASA taa

BRASA na-ejikọta ugbu a ebe mba 198 na Mmụta, Gọọmenti na Azụmahịa. BRASA Business nwere ngalaba 12 jikọtara ọnụ: ire ahịa na akwụkwọ nnata, CRM, ngwa ahịa, ndị ọrụ na ọzụzụ, ụgwọ ọrụ na ụlọ akụ, oge, nkwado ezinụlọ, ọrụ iwu, fọm gọọmenti, akụkọ na ebe ọrụ ọnụ. Ọ gụnyekwara akwụkwọ ndekọ ego mepere emepe, usoro ọrụ a na-eduzi na ọrụ na-aga n'ihu n'enweghị ịntanetị.

BRASA Financials depụtara usoro ịkwụ ụgwọ 385 sitere na ndekọ 395, na-anọchi anya nkọwa ahịa 161 na ụdị ịkwụ ụgwọ 216: ego ekwentị, nnyefe ozugbo, QR, obere akpa dijitalụ, netwọkụ ego, nnyefe ego, open banking, kaadị obodo na USSD.

Ịdị na katalọgụ apụtaghị na arụnyere ya. Ndị na-enye ọrụ nwere ikike na-ebuga ego; usoro ọ bụla chọrọ nyocha mba na isi mmalite, ikike, njikọta, ule mmezi na nkwenye mmepụta. BRASA na-ejikọta nhọrọ ndị a kwadoro na akwụkwọ nnata, mmezi na akwụkwọ ndekọ ego mepere emepe.
