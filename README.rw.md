# BRASA Business

** Porogaramu yubucuruzi yubuntu hamwe nisoko ryabaturage-byateguwe kuri buri bucuruzi. **

[Fungura BRASA Business](https://brasa.business/) · [BRASA World](https://brasa.world/) · [Uburezi](https://brasa.education/) · [Guverinoma](https://brasagovernment.net/)

BRASA Business butanga abenegihugu ibikoresho bifatika byo gutangiza, gukora, no guteza imbere ubucuruzi. Ihuriro rikubiyemo imyuga myinshi n’imirenge mu gihe hagumaho uburambe bujyanye n’uburezi, serivisi za leta, na buri rupapuro rw’igihugu.

## Ibyo abaturage bahabwa

- Porogaramu yubucuruzi yubuntu idafite ikigeragezo cyangwa abiyandikisha
- Ibikoresho byahujwe n'imyuga n'imirenge itandukanye
- Indangamuntu ya BRASA binyuze muri sisitemu iranga abenegihugu
- Kwishura abakiriya neza aho kuba BRASA ifata amafaranga yabacuruzi
- Guhuza imyigire, serivisi rusange, hamwe nisoko ryagutse rya BRASA

## Amahame yo gukora

- BRASA ntabwo isaba abaturage gutangira gukoresha software yubucuruzi.
- Indangamuntu ikorwa binyuze muri serivise isangiwe.
- BRASA ntabwo yicaye munzira yo kwishyura hagati yubucuruzi nabakiriya bayo.
- Kwishyura mu gihugu, imisoro, na fagitire bigomba kugenzurwa mbere yo gutangazwa.

## Kubona ururimi

Icyongereza gikomeza kuba isoko. Buri gisobanuro cyasohotse cyakira dosiye yacyo hamwe na URL rusange kugirango abaturage bashobore gukoresha BRASA mururimi rwabo badahinduye igipimo cyo guhindura icyongereza.

## Tangira umushinga

Sura [brasa.business](https://brasa.business/) kugira ngo ushakishe imyuga kandi ukore konti yawe.

## Kubika inyandiko

Urubuga rwoherejwe nka HTML ihagaze neza na JavaScript. Impinduka zigira umwirondoro, kwishura, inyemezabuguzi, cyangwa kubahiriza igihugu bisaba kugenzura mbere yo kurekurwa.

## BRASA uyu munsi

BRASA ubu ihuza aho ibihugu 198 bigana n'Uburezi, Guverinoma n'Ubucuruzi. BRASA Business ifite ibice 12 bihujwe: kugurisha n'inyemezabwishyu, CRM, ibarura, abakozi n'amahugurwa, imishahara na banki, igihe, ubufasha bw'umuryango, amategeko, impapuro za leta, raporo n'ahantu ho gukorera hamwe. Harimo kandi igitabo cy'ibaruramari gifunguye, inzira z'akazi ziyobowe no gukomeza gukora nta murandasi.

BRASA Financials ikataloge sisitemu 385 zo kwishyura zivuye mu nyandiko 395, zirimo ibisobanuro by'amasoko 161 n'ubwoko 216 bwo kwishyura: amafaranga ya telefoni, kohereza ako kanya, QR, amakofi y'ikoranabuhanga, imiyoboro y'amafaranga, kohereza amafaranga, open banking, amakarita yo mu gihugu na USSD.

Kuba muri kataloge ntibisobanura ko byatangiye gukora. Abatanga serivisi bafite uruhushya ni bo bimura amafaranga; buri buryo bugomba kugenzurwa ku gihugu n'inkomoko, uruhushya, ihuzwa, igerageza ry'ubwishyu n'icyemezo cyo gukoresha.
